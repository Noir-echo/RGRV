A WHOLE STUDIO IN YOUR POCKET.

Each block is a step: the orange one is the playhead.

1RACK

6-slot synth rack

Each slot holds a synth or a drum kit with 4 sound-shaping macros, drive, crush, tempo-synced delay and reverb.

2MIDI

Plays like real gear

Each rack listens to a MIDI channel. Whatever the sequencer, pads or controller send there plays the rack.

3SEQ

16 tracks · piano roll

1024 steps per track, chords, conditions, ratchets, and a piano roll to stamp notes and free chords onto steps.

4GEN

Auto · Euclid · Random

Generate basslines, acid, pads, riffs or euclidean rhythms, then let the racks play them.

5DSTR

Destroy Mode

An X/Y pad driving 9 live MIDI effects — repeat, stutter, echo, filter, tape stop, warp…

6CTRL

128 assignable CC

4 banks of 32 knobs and a fader. The default knobs already tweak the racks: cutoff, resonance, reverb…

7REC

Record to WAV

One tap on REC, one tap to stop, name your track and save it wherever you like.

8LINK

Bluetooth + USB MIDI

Keep driving your external machines alongside the built-in sounds. MIDI clock out included.

15 INSTRUMENTS. ZERO SAMPLES.

Every sound is generated live by code written for RGRV: nothing is sampled, and no brand names are used. Each instrument has 4 macros chosen to really transform the sound.
